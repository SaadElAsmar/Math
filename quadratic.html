Quadratic equation with a parameter

Discussion according to the real parameter m

(m + 1)x² + 2(m − 5)x + 4m − 5 = 0(E)
  1. 1)
    Solve (E) for m = −1.

    For m = −1, (E) becomes a first-degree equation:

    −12x − 9 = 0⇔x = −9/12 = −3/4
    Resultx = −3/4

    From now on, m ≠ −1.

  2. 2)
    For m ≠ −1, compute Δ', the product P and the sum S of the roots, then make the sign table of Δ', P and S.
    1

    Compute Δ'

    a = m + 1 ,  b' = m − 5 ,  c = 4m − 5

    Δ' = b'² − ac =(m − 5)² − (m + 1)(4m − 5) =m² − 10m + 25 − (4m² − m − 5) =−3m² − 9m + 30
    2

    Roots of Δ' (trinomial in m)

    A = −3 , B = −9 , C = 30

    A + B + C = 18 ≠ 0 and A − B + C = 36 ≠ 0: no shortcut.

    D = B² − 4AC = 81 + 360 = 441 √D = 21 m₁ = −B + √D2A = 9 + 21−6 = −5 m₂ = −B − √D2A = 9 − 21−6 = 2

    A = −3 < 0: Δ' has the sign of A outside the roots and the opposite sign between them.

    3

    P and S

    P = ca = 4m − 5m + 1 S = −2b'a = −2(m − 5)m + 1

    A quotient has the sign of the product of its numerator and denominator. 4m − 5 = 0 for m = 5/4 ; m − 5 = 0 for m = 5 ; m + 1 = 0 for m = −1 (forbidden value).

    4

    Sign table

  3. 3)
    Find m so that (E) has:
    1. a)
      two distinct roots x₁ and x₂.
      1

      Conditions

      m ≠ −1 and Δ' > 0

      2

      From the table

      Δ' > 0 ⇔ −5 < m < 2

      Resultm ∈ ]−5 ; −1[ ∪ ]−1 ; 2[
    2. b)
      a double root. Give it.
      1

      Condition

      Δ' = 0 ⇔ m = −5 or m = 2

      2

      The double root

      x₀ = −b'a = −(m − 5)m + 1 m = −5 :  x₀ = 10−4 = −5/2 m = 2 :  x₀ = 33 = 1

      Check: m = −5 gives −(2x + 5)² = 0 and m = 2 gives 3(x − 1)² = 0.

      Resultm = −5 : x₀ = −5/2
      m = 2 : x₀ = 1
    3. c)
      two distinct roots of the same sign. Specify the sign.
      1

      Conditions

      Δ' > 0 (part a) and P > 0

      2

      Superpose the conditions

      3

      Sign of the roots, from S

      On ]−5 ; −1[ : S < 0, both roots negative.

      On ]5/4 ; 2[ : S > 0, both roots positive.

      Resultm ∈ ]−5 ; −1[ ∪ ]5/4 ; 2[
    4. d)
      two distinct roots of opposite signs.
      1

      Condition

      P < 0. Then ac < 0, so Δ' = b'² − ac > 0 automatically.

      2

      Superpose the conditions

      Resultm ∈ ]−1 ; 5/4[
    5. e)
      two distinct roots satisfying:
      1. i)
        (x₁ + 1)(x₂ + 1) ≥ 5.
        1

        Express with S and P

        (x₁ + 1)(x₂ + 1) = P + S + 1 =(4m − 5) − 2(m − 5) + (m + 1)m + 1 =3m + 6m + 1
        2

        Solve the inequality

        3m + 6m + 1 ≥ 5 ⇔3m + 6 − 5(m + 1)m + 1 ≥ 0 ⇔−2m + 1m + 1 ≥ 0

        ⇔ −1 < m ≤ 1/2

        3

        Superpose with a)

        Resultm ∈ ]−1 ; 1/2]
      2. ii)
        1x₁ + 1x₂ < m.
        1

        Express with S and P

        We need x₁x₂ ≠ 0, so m ≠ 5/4.

        1x₁ + 1x₂ = SP = −2(m − 5)4m − 5
        2

        Solve the inequality

        −2(m − 5)4m − 5 < m ⇔−2m + 10 − m(4m − 5)4m − 5 < 0 ⇔−4m² + 3m + 104m − 5 < 0

        Numerator: A = −4 , B = 3 , C = 10. A + B + C = 9 ≠ 0 and A − B + C = 3 ≠ 0.

        D = 9 + 160 = 169 ,  √D = 13 m₁ = −3 + 13−8 = −5/4 m₂ = −3 − 13−8 = 2

        ⇔ m ∈ ]−5/4 ; 5/4[ ∪ ]2 ; +∞[

        3

        Superpose with a)

        Resultm ∈ ]−5/4 ; −1[ ∪ ]−1 ; 5/4[
      3. iii)
        Show that (4x₁ + 3)(4x₂ + 3) does not depend on m.
        1

        Expand

        (4x₁ + 3)(4x₂ + 3) = 16x₁x₂ + 12(x₁ + x₂) + 9 =16P + 12S + 9
        2

        Replace P and S

        16(4m − 5) − 24(m − 5) + 9(m + 1)m + 1 =64m − 80 − 24m + 120 + 9m + 9m + 1 =49m + 49m + 1 = 49(m + 1)m + 1
        Result(4x₁ + 3)(4x₂ + 3) = 49 for every m
    6. f)
      two distinct roots, both less than 1.
      1

      Conditions

      x₁ < 1 and x₂ < 1 ⇔ x₁ − 1 and x₂ − 1 are both negative:

      •Δ' > 0 •(x₁ − 1)(x₂ − 1) > 0 •(x₁ − 1) + (x₂ − 1) < 0
      2

      Express with S and P

      (x₁ − 1)(x₂ − 1) = P − S + 1 = 7m − 14m + 1 (x₁ − 1) + (x₂ − 1) = S − 2 = −4m + 8m + 1
      3

      Superpose the conditions

      Resultm ∈ ]−5 ; −1[
  4. 4)
    Find m so that −1 is a root of (E), then find the other root.
    1

    −1 is a root ⇔ a − b + c = 0

    (m + 1) − 2(m − 5) + 4m − 5 = 0 ⇔3m + 6 = 0 ⇔m = −2
    2

    The other root, from P

    (−1) · x₂ = P = 4(−2) − 5−2 + 1 = 13 ⇔x₂ = −13
    Resultm = −2 , x₂ = −13
  5. 5)
    Find m so that 0 is a root of (E), then find the other root.
    1

    0 is a root ⇔ c = 0

    4m − 5 = 0 ⇔ m = 5/4

    2

    The other root, from S

    0 + x₂ = S = −2(5/4 − 5)5/4 + 1 = 15/29/4 = 10/3
    Resultm = 5/4 , x₂ = 10/3

Remarks: m = 5/4 is the border between c) and d): one root is 0, so the roots are neither of the same sign nor of opposite signs. In e.iii), 4x + 3 = 0 gives x = −3/4, the root found in part 1 for m = −1.
