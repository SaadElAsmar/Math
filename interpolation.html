How to use
  • Double-tap empty space: add a point. Double-tap the bottom strip: add a break.
  • Drag points and breaks. Shift locks the direction.
  • Long-press or right-click a point, a break, a curve or a tangent for its menu.
  • Tap a dot at a break to make it f(c).
  • Tangent tool (curve icon): tap any curve to add a tangent, then drag its point along the curve.
  • Functions: type sin(x), 2x^3 - x, exp(-x^2), sqrt(x), ln(x), abs(x), pi, e…
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom; drag empty space to pan.
