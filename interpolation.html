A smooth curve through your points

C² quintic spline, monotone between points: every local extremum is one of your points.

Double-tap empty space to add a point, or the bottom strip to add a break. Drag points and breaks. Long-press or right-click for a menu. Tap a dot at a break to make it f(c). Long-press or right-click the curve to colour its group. Pinch or scroll to zoom; drag empty space to pan; Shift locks the drag direction.
